About LEAP

An independent public resource for AI literacy, developed by researchers and educators committed to making AI understandable for everyone.

LEAP is built and maintained by a small team of researchers, educators, and designers. We work in partnership with schools, community organizations, and universities across the country.

AI literacy is for everyone, not just computer scientists.

The people most affected by AI systems — students, parents, workers, voters — are rarely given the tools to understand or question them. We design for the person who didn't grow up with this, who doesn't have a CS background, who just wants to know what's happening and what to do about it.

Honest about what AI can and cannot do.

We resist both uncritical enthusiasm and reflexive fear. Our materials explain how AI systems actually work, what their limitations are, and why those limitations matter — in plain language, with real examples, without the hype.

Built with educators, not just for them.

Every classroom resource is developed in partnership with practicing teachers and tested in real classrooms before it appears on this site. We revise based on what actually works, not what sounds good in a workshop.

Free, open, and attribution-only.

All LEAP materials are freely available and openly licensed. We don't charge schools or families, and we don't carry advertising. We ask only that you credit LEAP when you share or adapt our materials.

LEAP conducts original research on how people understand and reason about AI. Our work informs our curriculum design and contributes to the broader field of AI literacy education.
