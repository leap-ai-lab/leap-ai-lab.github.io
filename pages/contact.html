Ways to work with LEAP

Request a workshop

We offer professional development sessions for schools, districts, and university departments. Sessions can be adapted to your context and audience.

Partner with us

We work with schools and community organizations to develop and pilot new curriculum. If you are interested in co-developing materials, reach out.

Share feedback

We revise our materials based on how they work in practice. If you've used something from LEAP and want to tell us what happened, we want to hear it.

Stay updated

We send occasional updates when new materials are released and events are announced. No more than once a month.
